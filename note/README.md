# The practitioner note

`open-tests.html` is a self-contained write-up aimed at someone who trades the
opening hour for a living: what we tested, how we defined each term, what we
found, and the ten errors we made getting there. It exists to be attacked —
the definitions section is the point, because a negative result may be a
verdict on our rule rather than on the read it was meant to mechanise.
